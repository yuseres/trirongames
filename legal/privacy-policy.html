Who We Are

The data controller for TrironGames applications is Claudiu Tanasa, an independent developer publishing under the TrironGames name. Where this policy says "we", "us" or "the Service Provider", it means him. Contact details are in Contact Us.

Information You Provide

The Application acquires information you supply when you download and register the Application. Registration is not mandatory, but some features may require it. The Service Provider may use this information to contact you with important service notices (for example about your account or a change to these documents). We do not send marketing messages to players under the age of digital consent.

Some Applications (including Color Boom and Animals Merge) include an online leaderboard feature. If you choose to set a display name there, that name and your score are stored and displayed publicly to other players within that Application. This information is transmitted to and processed by Unity Gaming Services (UGS), which operates the leaderboard, cloud-save and sign-in infrastructure for Applications that use it. Please do not use your real name or other personal information in a display name; it is visible to other players. You can change your display name at any time from within the Application, where available. For users below the age of digital consent, the display name (and, where the friends feature is used, friend connections) is the only player-provided personal information collected, and it is used solely to operate the leaderboard and friends features.

Animals Merge also includes a friends feature. Each player has a friend code made of their display name and a number. If you share your code, the person who enters it can send you a friend request; if you accept, you and that player can see each other's display name and weekly leaderboard score. The Application has no chat or free-text messaging. Your friend list and pending friend requests are stored and processed by Unity Gaming Services (UGS). You can remove a friend at any time from within the Application.

Information Collected Automatically

The Application may collect certain information automatically, including but not limited to:

  • The type of mobile device you use
  • Your mobile device's unique device ID
  • The IP address of your mobile device
  • Your mobile operating system
  • The type of mobile Internet browser you use
  • Information about the way you use the Application

For general-audience users, this may include your Google Advertising ID (GAID) or an equivalent identifier, used by advertising and analytics partners to personalize ads and measure ad performance — this is separate from your device's hardware identifiers and can be reset or limited from your device's ad settings.

For users identified as below the age of digital consent, the Advertising ID is not collected or transmitted, advertising is limited to non-personalized (contextual) ads, and analytics is limited to aggregate measurement with no advertising or cross-session identifier. See Children's Privacy.

Advertising Freemium

The Application displays advertisements served by third-party ad networks. For general-audience users these networks may use device identifiers, cookies, and similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your interests and usage. The following ad service provider may collect data:

  • Google AdMob

Ad formats used are banner ads, opt-in rewarded ads (which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit), and, for general-audience users only, ads shown when returning to the app. There are no interstitial (unskippable full-screen) ads.

Where required by applicable law (including EU/UK GDPR and ePrivacy rules), the Application requests your consent — through a certified consent-management platform (Google's User Messaging Platform) — before enabling personalized advertising or non-essential ad-related data processing. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time through the in-app privacy settings or your device's ad settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For users below the age of digital consent, advertising is restricted to non-personalized (contextual) banner and opt-in rewarded ads only, rated for a general audience; no return-to-app ads are shown, no Advertising ID is used, and no behavioral or interest-based profiling is performed. All ads are served by SDKs that Google self-certifies as suitable for Families-policy apps.

In-App Purchases Freemium

The Application offers optional in-app purchases processed securely through Google Play. The Service Provider does not collect or store your payment information — all transactions are handled by Google Play's payment system under their own privacy policy and terms. The Application does record which items you have purchased (your purchase history for this Application) so it can grant and restore the corresponding in-game content; this is tied to your Google Play and game account, not to a payment instrument.

All purchases are subject to Google Play's Terms of Service. Refund requests are subject to Google Play's refund policy.

Location

Third-Party Access

The Application relies on the third-party services below, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy. Some of these services receive data that can identify your device or session (for example an advertising identifier, an app-instance identifier, or a crash report); this is not limited to aggregated or anonymized data. For users below the age of digital consent, the ad-related identifiers and personalization signals described below are disabled.

  • Google Play Services — core Android services, integrity, and (for Applications that use it) Google Play Games sign-in, which associates an anonymous game account with your Google account so your progress can follow you across devices.
  • Google AdMob — serves the Application's ads. For general-audience users it may collect your Advertising ID and work with a broader network of third-party ad-technology partners to serve and measure ads; a current list of AdMob's partners is at support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903. Consent is gathered through Google's User Messaging Platform where required.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics (both provided by Google) — analytics (aggregate gameplay and engagement events, session length, device model and operating system, an app-instance identifier) and crash and stability reporting (crash stack traces, device state at the time of a crash, and a crash-instance identifier). For users below the age of digital consent, Firebase runs in a consent-restricted mode with advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization all denied — measurement is cookieless and carries no advertising identifier.
  • Unity Gaming Services (UGS) — provides sign-in, cloud save, and (for Applications with a leaderboard) the leaderboard. Stores your saved game progress, an anonymous UGS player identifier, and — if you set one — your public display name and score, and your friend list and pending friend requests.
  • Unity Technologies — the Unity engine and its editor/runtime services. Unity may collect standard operational and diagnostic data as described in its policy.

The Service Provider may disclose your information:

  • As required by law (e.g. to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process)
  • When necessary to protect rights, safety, or investigate fraud
  • With trusted service providers who have agreed to adhere to this privacy statement

The Service Provider does not sell your personal information to third parties.

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, the Service Provider processes your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide the core features of the Application you use, such as saving your progress or, where available, displaying a leaderboard.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Application secure, prevent fraud, and understand how the Application is used so it can be improved.
  • Consent — for personalized advertising, where required by your device settings or applicable law.
  • Legal obligation — where processing is required to comply with the law.

Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or a jurisdiction with similar protections, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data the Service Provider holds about you
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data (see Data Retention & Account Deletion)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (e.g. personalized ads)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Contact Us. We may need to verify your identity first (for example by asking for your in-app Account ID). We respond within one month, extendable by up to two further months for complex requests, as GDPR allows.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, and disclosed
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — the Service Provider does not sell your personal information, and does not knowingly share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond what is described in the Advertising section
  • Non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights

You can submit a request by contacting us using the details in Contact Us; we aim to respond within 45 days. The categories of personal information we collect, and why, are listed in Information You Provide, Information Collected Automatically and Data at a Glance.

Do Not Track

Some mobile operating systems and browsers include a "Do Not Track" feature. Because there is no consistent industry standard for how such signals should be interpreted, the Application does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals (it is a mobile app, not a website, and does not read browser-level signals such as Global Privacy Control). You can still control ad personalization through your device's ad settings as described in the Advertising section.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by uninstalling the Application using the standard uninstall process on your mobile device or via the application marketplace. Uninstalling does not delete data already stored on our service providers' servers; to remove it, delete your account first (see Data Retention & Account Deletion). You can also reset your advertising ID, opt out of ad personalization, or change your consent choice at any time, as described in Advertising.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

User-provided data (such as your display name and saved game progress) is retained for as long as your account exists and is deleted when you delete your account (in the app, after a 7-day grace period during which you can cancel; by email, within 7 business days). Analytics event data is retained by Google Analytics for Firebase for up to 14 months; crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Data held by advertising partners is retained under their own policies. We keep a minimal record that a deletion request was made only as long as needed to show we complied.

You can request deletion of your account and data at any time — either directly in the app (profile icon → Account → Delete Account) or by email. Full instructions, including what to include in your email so we can locate your account quickly, are on our Account Deletion page.

Children's Privacy

Animals Merge is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Color Boom is directed to a general (adult) audience.

"Age of digital consent." This is the age below which a country requires a parent's consent for processing a child's personal data — between 13 and 16 in the EEA and UK, and 13 in the United States. Because we cannot reliably tell which country's threshold applies, we treat every player under 16, and every player whose age we do not know, as below it.

Default protective settings. Players aged 13–15, and any player who skips or does not complete the age screen, receive our most protective settings by default. For these players, the Application:

  • does not collect or transmit the Google Advertising ID or any equivalent advertising identifier;
  • serves only non-personalized, contextual ads (banner and opt-in rewarded), rated for a general audience, through SDKs that Google self-certifies for Families-policy apps — no return-to-app ads, no interstitials, and no behavioral or interest-based advertising;
  • runs analytics and crash reporting in a consent-restricted mode: advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalization are all denied, so measurement is aggregate and cookieless with no advertising or cross-session identifier;
  • limits the personal information collected to what is needed to operate the game — saved game progress, an anonymous account identifier and, only if the player chooses to set them, a public display name and friend connections (no free-text messages are exchanged between players);
  • does not ask for or use precise location, contacts, the camera, the microphone, or any special-category data.

COPPA. If the Service Provider becomes aware that it has inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, it will delete it as soon as reasonably possible. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information to the Application, and want it reviewed or removed, should contact trirongames@gmail.com or use the Account Deletion page.

Parental controls. Parents and guardians can further limit or reset the advertising identifier and ad personalization from the device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and can manage or remove a Google Play Games / Google account link from their Google account settings.
